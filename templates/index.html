Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding admissions, affiliation, cutoffs, and quotas at RVITM.

What is the affiliation and accreditation of RVITM?

R.V Institute of Technology and Management (RVITM) is approved by AICTE, New Delhi and is affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi. It is established under the renowned Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST).

How does the placement process work at RVITM?

Placements at RVITM are centralized through the RV Group placement cell. Students have access to identical placement recruitment opportunities, hackathons, and top recruiters like Microsoft, Amazon, Cisco, and Bosch.

What are the eligibility criteria for B.E. programs?

Candidates must have passed 10+2 / Pre-University (PUC) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry / Biotechnology / Computer Science, securing a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for Karnataka SC/ST/OBC candidates).

How can I enquire for Management Quota seats?

You can submit the online enquiry form on this page or consult our AI Admission Advisor. Our admissions office in JP Nagar, Bengaluru will contact you with specific branch availability and fee schedules.

Why is email verification required before using the AI Chatbot?

We require your email address so our AI advisor can deliver personalized branch brochures, cutoffs, and scholarship evaluations directly to your inbox while ensuring priority counseling support.
